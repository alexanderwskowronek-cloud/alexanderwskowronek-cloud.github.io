Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · applies to the iPhone app Alpano

In short: Alpano collects no data. The app has no account, no server of its own, no advertising and no analytics. Peak identification runs entirely on your iPhone.

2. What is processed on your iPhone

Alpano uses the following data on the device only. None of it is sent to us or to third parties.

  • Location – if you allow it, Alpano uses it to work out which peaks are visible from where you stand. It is not stored. Without it you can choose viewpoints and places in Panorama mode.
  • Camera – in AR mode the camera image is the background for the peak names. It is not recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Motion and compass – the orientation of your iPhone is evaluated while you use the app so that the names appear in the right place. It is not stored.
  • Settings and a usage counter – such as units, the language of peak names, the last viewpoint and a count of days of use. They stay in the app's storage on your iPhone.

3. Map

In Map mode Alpano loads the base map from Apple Maps (MapKit). Your iPhone fetches map data from Apple's servers, and Apple processes the connection data this requires under its own responsibility. Alpano passes no data to us in the process. See Apple's privacy policy. Peak identification in AR and Panorama needs no connection.

4. No third parties, no tracking

Alpano contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. There is no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Legal basis, retention, recipients

Location and camera are used on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give in the iOS permission dialog and can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings. Settings remain on your iPhone until you delete the app. We have no recipients and make no transfers to third countries.

6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we store no data about you, we hold no personal data to disclose.

7. Changes

If what the app processes changes, we will update this page and the date above.

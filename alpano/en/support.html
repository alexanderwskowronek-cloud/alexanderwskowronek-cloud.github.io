Support

Help for the iPhone app Alpano

Contact: Alpano is developed and maintained by one person. Send an email to support@larkminds.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and Alpano version – you find it in the app under Settings. You will usually get a reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Which regions does Alpano know peaks for?

The Alps. Peak data and terrain are included in the app.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for peak recognition and the panorama; both are computed entirely on your iPhone. Only the base map in map mode comes from Apple Maps and needs a connection.

The names appear next to the mountains. What can I do?

This is usually the compass. Keep your iPhone away from metal, cars and magnets; cases or mounts with magnets disturb the compass. Move the iPhone in a figure-eight a few times to let the compass recalibrate.

Does Alpano work without location access?

Yes. In panorama mode you can choose viewpoints, or long-press the map to set a place. You can change location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Where do the peak names come from?

From OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). All sources and licences are listed in the app under Settings › Sources and licences. If a peak is missing or a name is wrong, please send an email.

What data does Alpano collect?

None. See the privacy policy for details.

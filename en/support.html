Support

How can we help?

Most answers are below or on the app’s help page. If yours isn’t, write to support@larkminds.com and tell us the app, your device and the iOS version. Every reply comes from a person.

Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app and tap “Restore Purchases” in the settings. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. You won’t be charged again.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

Where is an app’s privacy policy?

On the app’s help page, linked below. The App Store also lists what data an app handles on its page under “App Privacy”.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Please write to us. A short description helps most: what you did, what happened and what you expected. A screenshot is welcome.

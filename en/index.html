Five tools

Identify mountains, understand your sleep, plan lessons, follow the moon, keep score in tennis and darts: each app does one job.

  • AlpanoMountain identifier

    Point your iPhone at the mountains and Alpano names the peaks live in the camera view, offline too.

    Get it on the App Store – Alpano Learn more about Alpano →

  • DawnlineSleep analysis

    Sleep analysis for iPhone and Apple Watch: sleep stages, a sleep score and a diary, with the working behind every number.

    Get it on the App Store – Dawnline Learn more about Dawnline →

  • PaukerTeacher planner

    A teacher planner for iPhone and Apple Watch: timetable, appointments, lesson notes, class lists and grades in one place. In German.

    Get it on the App Store – Pauker Learn more about Pauker →

  • ScoryTennis score counter

    Get it on the App Store – Scory Learn more about Scory →

  • Darts Scoreboard ProDarts scorer

    A darts scorer for iPhone and iPad: counts 501, 301 and Cricket, calls the scores and shows the way to the checkout.

    Get it on the App Store – Darts Scoreboard Pro Learn more about Darts Scoreboard Pro →

  • LunifyMoon calendar

    A moon calendar for iPhone and Apple Watch: moon phase, moonrise and moonset for your location, full and new moons at a glance for the month. Calculated on the device from astronomical formulas.

    Get it on the App Store – Lunify

Approach

  • Useful

    A good app serves one task. Whatever distracts from it, we leave out.

  • Honest

    A good app doesn’t pretend to do more than it can. It promises only what it delivers, and every number can be traced.

  • Lasting

    A good app follows no fashion. We look after it for years instead of constantly reinventing it.

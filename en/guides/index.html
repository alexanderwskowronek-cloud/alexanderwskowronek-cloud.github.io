Let the app do the sums

Darts Scoreboard Pro (on the App Store as “Darts Scoreboard Pro: Counter”) is a darts scoring app for iPhone and iPad by an independent developer. It keeps score in X01 and Cricket, calls the scores and works out the checkout. If you go bust, it says so before the next dart flies. There are no ads and no account, and the app collects no data.

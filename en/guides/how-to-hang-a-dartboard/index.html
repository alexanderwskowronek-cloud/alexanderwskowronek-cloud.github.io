How to hang a dartboard: height and distance

Last updated: November 2026

The centre of the bullseye hangs 1.73 m (5 ft 8 in) above the floor. The throwing line (oche) is 2.37 m (7 ft 9¼ in) from the face of the board, measured horizontally. To check both at once, measure the diagonal from the centre of the bull to the oche at floor level: it is 2.93 m (9 ft 7⅜ in). These measurements apply to steel-tip darts under World Darts Federation rules.

What are the official dartboard measurements?

MeasurementMetricImperialMeasured
Height1.73 m5 ft 8 infrom the floor (at oche level) to the centre of the bullseye
Throwing distance2.37 m7 ft 9¼ inhorizontally from the face of the board to the oche
Diagonal2.93 m9 ft 7⅜ infrom the centre of the bullseye to the oche at floor level

The rules define the metric values. The imperial values are the usual conversions and differ by a few millimetres: 5 ft 8 in is 1.727 m and 7 ft 9¼ in is 2.369 m. Either set works; just don't mix them. The diagonal is your check: by Pythagoras, √(1.73² + 2.37²) ≈ 2.934 m, and √(68² + 93.25²) ≈ 115.4 in, which is 9 ft 7⅜ in. If the diagonal is right, height and distance fit together.

How do I hang a dartboard step by step?

  1. Choose the spot: You need at least 2.37 m (7 ft 9¼ in) of clear floor space in front of the wall up to the oche, plus room to stand behind it. A spot away from doors and walkways is safer.
  2. Mark the height: Measure 1.73 m (5 ft 8 in) up from the floor and draw a level line. The centre of the bullseye goes on this line, not the top of the board.
  3. Fit the bracket: If the bracket sits exactly in the centre of the back of the board, it goes on the line. If not, measure the offset between bracket and bullseye on the board and move your mark by that amount.
  4. Hang and align the board: The 20 sits at the top centre and, on tournament boards, is the darker of the two segment colours.
  5. Mark the oche: Drop a plumb line from the face of the board to the floor and measure 2.37 m (7 ft 9¼ in) horizontally into the room. Put the tape line or oche there.
  6. Check the diagonal: Measure from the centre of the bullseye to the oche at floor level. At 2.93 m (9 ft 7⅜ in), everything fits.

Where exactly is the oche measured?

At the edge where your toes are. When throwing, your feet must stay behind the oche and must not step on it. If you throw from beside the oche, your feet must stay behind its imaginary extension (WDF rules 6.04 and 6.05). If the board's face sticks out from the wall, for example in a cabinet, measure from the face of the board, not from the wall.

Does this apply to soft-tip darts?

The measurements on this page are for steel-tip darts under WDF rules. Soft-tip darts follow the rules of the respective league or machine operator. Check your league's rule book.

How big is a regulation dartboard?

Under WDF specifications, a tournament bristle board has an overall diameter of 451 mm (± 3 mm), about 17.75 in. From the centre of the bull to the outer edge of the double wire it is 170 mm.

  • PDC Europe: rules overview (German): bullseye at 1.73 m, oche 2.37 m from the front of the board

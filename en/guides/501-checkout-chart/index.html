501 checkout chart: every finish from 170 to 2

Last updated: November 2026

In 501 with a double finish, every score from 2 to 170 can be checked out in one visit, except 159, 162, 163, 165, 166, 168 and 169. The highest checkout is 170 (T20 · T20 · Bull). The chart below gives a verified route for every score and the minimum number of darts it needs.

Which scores can you check out in 501?

With three darts, every score from 2 to 170 can be checked out except 159, 162, 163, 165, 166, 168 and 169. The highest checkout is 170 with T20 · T20 · Bull. The last dart must always land in a double or the bullseye, because with a double finish only a dart that brings the score to exactly zero in a double counts.

How many darts do you need at least?

  • One dart: every even score from 2 to 40 (D1 to D20) and 50 (Bull).
  • Two darts: up to 110 (T20 · Bull). Below 110, the scores 99, 102, 103, 105, 106, 108 and 109 cannot be finished with two darts, only with three.
  • Three darts: up to 170. Above that, no score can be finished in one visit.

Why are 159, 162, 163, 165, 166, 168 and 169 impossible?

Two darts score at most 120 (T20 · T20), and the highest double is the bull at 50. For these seven scores there is no combination of two darts and a double that adds up exactly. For 169, for example, T20 · T20 leaves 49, which is not a double; T20 · T19 leaves 52 and T20 · Bull leaves 59, neither of which is a double either. A script checked all combinations.

How was the chart checked?

A script went through every combination of up to three darts (singles 1–20, 25, doubles 1–20, Bull, trebles 1–20) for every score from 2 to 170 and counted only routes whose last dart is a double or the bull. For each route in the chart it verified that the sum equals the score exactly and that the last dart is a double. The double-finish rule itself is WDF rule 2.02; the bull counts as double 25 under rule 2.04.

The full rules are in the guide 501 darts rules: double out and bust.

Sources

  • World Darts Federation (WDF): Playing and Tournament Rules, 20th revised edition, 28 February 2018: rules 2.02 (double finish), 2.04 (bull counts as double 25), 2.05 (bust)
  • Own calculation: complete enumeration of all combinations of up to three darts for every score from 2 to 170; every route checked for sum and final double (script en_guides.py)

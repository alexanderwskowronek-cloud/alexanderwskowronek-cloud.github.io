Klarton

Water in the speaker? Klarton plays it out.

Water in the speaker sounds as if someone had put a cushion in front of it. Klarton plays a low tone whose vibration pushes the droplets out of the grille.

View on the App Store

Klarton is an iPhone app by an independent developer that plays a low tone to push water out of the speaker grille and measures how much of the sound gets through before and after.

How it works

One tap, fifteen seconds

One tap starts a run. You hold the phone at a slight angle with the speaker pointing down, and fifteen seconds later it is over.

Measured before and after

Klarton plays test tones and listens through the microphone to how loudly they arrive. The comparison shows whether more of the tone gets through after a run.

Your device's own frequency

The often quoted 165 hertz is only an average. Klarton looks for the frequency at which your device resonates most strongly and uses it for the water mode.

Dust and fine control

A mode against dust in the grille, a gentle sweep from 60 to 400 hertz and a manual setting from 60 hertz to 20 kilohertz.

Microphone for measuring only

Measuring needs the microphone, and nothing else does. What arrives there stays in memory, is never stored and never leaves the device. There are no ads, no account and no sign-up.

Honest about the limits

Klarton helps with water in the speaker openings. Water inside the device is a case for a service appointment, not for a tone. How much one run achieves depends on the device and on the amount of water.

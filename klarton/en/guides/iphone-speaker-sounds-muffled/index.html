iPhone speaker sounds muffled: causes and fixes

Last updated: November 2026

A muffled iPhone speaker usually has one of three causes: water in the grille, dirt in the opening, or a case or screen film covering the opening. Remove the case and film first, clean the opening with a small, soft, clean and dry brush, and let a wet phone dry. If the sound stays muffled, the phone needs service.

What are the most common causes?

CauseHow to recognize itWhat you can do
Water in the grilleThe phone got wet; the sound is dull or crackly.Let it dry, place it speaker side down on a cloth, help with a low tone.
Dirt or dust in the openingLint or residue is visible in the speaker opening.Clean carefully with a small, soft-bristled brush that is clean and dry.
Case or screen filmThe sound improves when you take the case off.Remove the case, film and screen protector to test.
Sound is muted or going elsewhereNo sound from the speaker at all.Check Silent mode and Do Not Disturb.
Faulty speakerStill no sound or distorted sound after drying and cleaning.Contact service.

How do I check whether the speaker itself is the problem?

  1. Make sure Silent mode and Do Not Disturb are off.
  2. Remove the case, screen protector and any film.
  3. Look at the speaker opening at the bottom and the receiver opening at the top to see whether anything blocks them.
  4. Go to Settings › Sounds & Haptics and drag the Ringtone and Alerts slider back and forth a few times. If you hear nothing, or the speaker button on the slider is dimmed, the speaker might need service.

Is only one of the two speakers muffled?

On an iPhone, sound comes from the bottom speaker and from the receiver at the top. If only one side sounds dull, the problem usually sits in that opening, for example a droplet or dirt. Klarton's measurement checks whether one of the two speakers sounds muffled.

How can I tell whether cleaning helped?

Without a measuring device, your ear is the judge. Play the same track at the same volume before and after drying. Klarton plays test tones and measures through the microphone how loudly they arrive, once before and once after. There is no part inside a phone that measures water in the speaker chamber directly.

Sources

  • Apple Support: If you hear no sound or distorted sound from your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch speaker: Silent mode, Do Not Disturb, remove cases and films, check the speaker and receiver openings, clean with a small, soft-bristled, clean and dry brush, Ringtone and Alerts slider test, service
  • Apple Support: About splash, water, and dust resistance of iPhone 7 and later: water might degrade speaker or microphone performance until it evaporates

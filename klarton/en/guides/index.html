With Klarton

Klarton is an iPhone app by an independent developer that plays a low tone to push water out of the speaker grille and measures how much of the sound gets through before and after. One tap starts a fifteen-second run. Klarton helps with water in the speaker openings. Water inside the device is a case for a service appointment.

How to get water out of your iPhone speaker

Last updated: November 2026

Wipe the iPhone with a soft, lint-free cloth, tap it gently against your hand with the connector facing down, and leave it in a dry place with some airflow. A low tone can also help push droplets out of the speaker grille. Skip the hair dryer, compressed air and rice, and don't charge it with a cable until it is dry.

How do I get water out of my iPhone speaker?

  1. Wipe it off: Use a soft, lint-free cloth, for example a lens cloth.
  2. Tap it out: Hold the iPhone with the connector facing down and tap it gently against your hand to remove excess liquid.
  3. Check for water: Place the iPhone speaker side down on a lint-free cloth. If water drips out, there is still water in the speaker.
  4. Let it dry: Leave it in a dry area with some airflow. Apple notes that a fan blowing cool air into the connector might help. Fully drying can take up to 24 hours.
  5. Optionally, use a tone: A low tone makes the speaker vibrate and can push droplets out of the grille. Hold the iPhone at a slight angle with the speaker pointing down.
  6. Charge only when dry: Apple recommends waiting at least 5 hours before charging with a Lightning or USB-C cable or connecting an accessory.

Why does my speaker sound muffled after getting wet?

According to Apple, water in the port might degrade speaker or microphone performance until it completely evaporates. The grille in front of the speaker is fine, and a droplet in it dampens the sound like a cushion.

Does a sound really push water out of a speaker?

The idea comes from the Apple Watch: when you turn off Water Lock, the watch plays a series of tones to clear water that remains in its speaker. Apps like Klarton apply the same principle to the iPhone. How much a run achieves depends on the device and the amount of water. A tone cannot reach water inside the device.

Which frequency works best?

165 hertz is often quoted. That is an average across many device generations; each device resonates most strongly at a slightly different frequency. Klarton measures that frequency through the microphone and uses it for its water mode if it clearly stands out.

What should I avoid?

No hair dryer or other heat source, no compressed air, no rice and no cotton swabs in the openings. The reasons are in the guide Wet iPhone: what not to do.

When does the iPhone need service?

When the sound stays muffled after drying, the speaker stops working, or the phone was submerged for a long time. Apple points out that liquid damage is not covered under warranty, but you might have rights under consumer law.
